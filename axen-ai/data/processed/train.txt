AXEN AI is a local artificial intelligence project.

AXEN AI is designed to run locally on a personal computer.

The goal of AXEN AI is to understand text and generate text.

Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science.

Machine learning allows computers to learn patterns from data.

A neural network contains layers of mathematical operations.

A Transformer uses attention mechanisms to process sequences.

Self-attention allows a model to determine relationships between tokens.

A token is a unit of text processed by a language model.

Training adjusts the parameters of a neural network to reduce prediction error.

The training process uses a loss function to measure prediction error.

Backpropagation calculates gradients of the loss with respect to model parameters.

The optimizer uses those gradients to update the model.

AXEN AI uses a Transformer architecture.

AXEN AI uses its own tokenizer.

AXEN AI runs locally without requiring a cloud AI API.

The long-term goal of AXEN AI is to become a capable local AI assistant.
